40/15 · Tennis Scoreboard

Keep score on your wrist. Relive every match on iPhone.

40/15 is a tennis scorekeeper for Apple Watch and iPhone. Tap once per point on your wrist — games, sets, tiebreaks, and serve order are tracked for you. When the match ends it syncs to your iPhone with score-growth charts, momentum, and per-point effort.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need my iPhone nearby to score? No — score entirely on Apple Watch. Matches sync to iPhone when it's next available.
  • Where do heart rate and calories come from? From an Apple Watch workout session during the match. Grant Health access when asked.
  • Can I end a match early? Yes — open the stats page during a match and tap Finish Match.
  • What formats are supported? One set, best of three, or best of five, with tiebreak, super-tiebreak, advantage, and no-ad options.
