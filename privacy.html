Privacy Policy

Forty Fifteen · Last updated 17 August 2026

Short version: Forty Fifteen does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Match and sensor data stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud container. We do not run data-collection servers.

Data the app uses

Match data, including scores, points, duration, opponents you name, optional venue, court surface, and match settings, is stored locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. It is used to show match history, statistics, and reports.

Health data, including heart rate, active energy, and workout duration, is read from an Apple Watch workout through Apple HealthKit with your permission. It is used only for live, match-level, and score-linked effort features. If you choose to save the workout, match workout data is written to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared or sold. You can revoke Health access at any time in the Health app or Settings.

Location is optional. If you allow it, Apple Watch records the GPS location where a match is played so it can be shown on a map and labelled with a venue. The location is stored with the match. You can score matches without granting location access.

Photos access is optional. If you tap Save to Photos, the iPhone app requests add-only access and writes the match-report image you selected. The app does not read or browse your photo library.

iCloud and device sync

Finished matches sync between your Apple Watch and iPhone using Apple's device connectivity. If you are signed into iCloud, match history, opponent data, recent-opponent order, and remembered match formats may be stored in the app's private iCloud container to sync across your own devices and restore on a new device. We cannot access that private container. If you use iCloud device backup, locally stored app data may also be included under Apple's terms.

Purchases

Forty Fifteen Pro is available as an auto-renewing monthly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase through Apple. Purchases, subscription renewals, receipts, and payment details are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple processes purchase data.

No accounts, advertising, or tracking

The app has no user accounts and requires no login. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. It communicates only with Apple services needed for iCloud sync, device connectivity, HealthKit, and App Store purchases.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Health, Location, or Photos access in Settings. You can delete a match in the app to remove it from your device. Deleting the app removes locally stored data from that device. Data in your private iCloud container can be removed through the app or by disabling iCloud for Forty Fifteen. Health data saved to Apple Health is managed by you in the Health app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
