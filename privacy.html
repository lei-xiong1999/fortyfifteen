Privacy Policy

40/15 · Tennis Scoreboard · Last updated 13 July 2026

Short version: 40/15 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account — never on our servers.

What the app stores

Match scores and settings you enter are stored locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Finished matches are synced between your own Apple Watch and iPhone using Apple's on-device connectivity.

If you are signed into iCloud, your match history and opponent list are also stored in the app's private iCloud container so they stay in sync across your own iPhones and can be restored on a new device. This data lives in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy — we have no access to it, and it is never sent to us or to any third-party server. If you are not signed into iCloud, the app simply keeps this data on your device. You can turn syncing off at any time by disabling iCloud Drive for 40/15 in iOS Settings.

Health data

If you grant permission, during a match the app reads your heart rate and active energy (calories) from an Apple Watch workout session to show live and post-match statistics, and — only if you choose to save — records the match as a workout in Apple Health. This health data is used solely to provide those features on your device. It is never transmitted off your device, never used for advertising, and never shared or sold. You can revoke Health access at any time in the iOS Health app or Settings.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no user accounts and no login. It contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. It makes no network requests to our servers or to any third party; the only off-device communication is with Apple's iCloud, to sync your own data across your devices as described above.

Data you can delete

You can delete any match from the history at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored data from that device; data stored in iCloud can be removed by deleting it in the app or by turning off iCloud Drive for 40/15 in iOS Settings. Health data saved to Apple Health is managed by you in the Health app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
